# Style notes (own words; from style_raw_notes.md, 17 ML/glaucoma papers)
- Title: ~14 words (13-16); often "Topic: Design label" with a colon (9/17); include disease, intervention/test, design.
- Abstract: structured Introduction / Methods / Results / Conclusion(s), 250-300 words, no references, numbers with CIs.
- Keywords: 3-10, alphabetical.
- Key Summary Points: 5 one-sentence bullets under "Why carry out this study?" (2-3) and "What was learned from the study?" (2-3).
- Introduction: 3-5 paragraphs, ~17 sentences; opens with disease burden / clinical need (9/17); ends with aim;
  the journal's instructions ask for an explicit testable hypothesis (only 2/17 do it -> we will, to comply).
- Sentences: mean ~24 words in the journal; we target <= 20 words, active voice, one idea per sentence.
- Sections: Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusion(s); Methods has sub-headings (data source, participants,
  outcomes, models, statistics, ethics). Ethics statement appears in Methods and in Declarations.
- Limitations: paragraph near the end of Discussion, ordinal ("First,... Second,..."), honest and specific.
- Conclusions: one short paragraph (~100 words), inference not summary, calls for prospective validation.
- References: Vancouver, numbered [n] in order of first citation.
- Figures: "Fig. N" with long descriptive captions (~45 words) that define abbreviations; tables with short titles and
  footnotes for abbreviations/statistics.
- Declarations: Funding, Medical Writing/Editorial Assistance, Author Contributions, Disclosures, Compliance with Ethics
  Guidelines, Data Availability.
