# Journal retargeting after two rejections (2026-11-25)

## Why the paper was rejected
The manuscript (a glaucoma visual-field progression study) was sent to the *International Journal of Forecasting* and
the *Journal of Operations Management*. Both publish forecasting-methodology and operations-management research. The
comments "not suitable for our journal / not at our journal's level / limited novelty" are, first of all, a SCOPE
rejection: neither journal's readership evaluates glaucoma progression detectors. Adding novelty cannot fix a scope
mismatch. The paper must go to a vision-science / ophthalmology journal.

## What changed in the paper (amendment C, pre-registered 2026-11-25 before running; commit a827dcc)
1. Clinic-noise null (label-free threshold source from detrended residual resampling) vs weekly retests vs shuffled records.
2. 6x larger evaluation: 690 eyes (150 progressors / 164 stable / 376 indeterminate) with 5-fold patient cross-fitting
   (previously 26 / 26).
3. New moderated (empirical-Bayes variance-borrowing) trend test D11 - honest negative result in real eyes.
4. Semi-synthetic truth with known loss (global / localised; 0.5, 1, 2 dB/yr).
5. Budgeted sequential monitoring (cumulative false-alarm control over 6 reviews) vs naive and confirmation rules.
Hypotheses: HC2, HC4, HC5 supported; HC1 partly (SC part failed); HC3 (new test beats D1) NOT supported. All reported.
