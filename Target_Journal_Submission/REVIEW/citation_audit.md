# Citation audit: manuscript_filled.md

Audited 2026-11-23. Source checked: `Target_Journal_Submission/manuscript/manuscript_filled.md` (36 references).

**Method.** For each DOI, metadata and abstract were taken from Crossref, OpenAlex (abstract_inverted_index), Europe PMC (core) and PubMed E-utilities. Abstracts were found for all 36 references (ref 24 is a correction notice with no abstract). Where the abstract did not settle a claim, the open-access full text was read through PMC E-utilities. This was done for ref 4 (PMC4082764), ref 5 (PMC2564806), ref 25 (PMC8742531) and ref 26 (PMC10404253). No publisher pages were used.

**Verdicts.**
- **SUPPORTED:** the abstract (or the open-access full text, where stated) says what the sentence claims.
- **PARTLY:** the source is relevant, but the sentence states more than the source says, or states it differently.
- **NOT SUPPORTED:** the source does not say what the sentence claims, or contradicts it.
- **UNVERIFIABLE:** no abstract or full text was available.
